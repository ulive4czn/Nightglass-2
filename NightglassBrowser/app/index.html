Privacy at a glance

Temporary session Cookies, site storage and cache use an in-memory browser profile and are cleared when the app fully closes.

Basic tracker filter A built-in list blocks requests to some known advertising and analytics domains. It is not a complete blocker.

Permissions denied by default Camera, microphone, location and notifications are denied by this starter build.

This does not hide your IP address or make you anonymous. Websites, your ISP, employer/school network, and DNS provider may still observe connections. Use HTTPS, keep Windows updated, and avoid installing unknown extensions or downloading untrusted files.
